Sensory Science Facility

Comprehensive consumer, analytical, and instrumental testing services for academic and industry partners. Reliable data for basic research, product development, and quality evaluation.

The Texas A&M Sensory Science Facility (SSF), directed by Dr. Robert Pellegrino, provides consumer, analytical, and instrumental testing services to academic and industry partners. Our facility is equipped with a full kitchen, laboratory, and 30 individual sensory booths.

Prospective clients are encouraged to schedule a free consultation to discuss project goals, methodology, timeline, and budget.

Consumer Testing

Testing with target or general consumer populations to generate statistically robust data on product acceptance, preference, and perception.

Standard Consumer Test

Full-scale evaluation with up to 100 consumers and up to 6 products.

Up to 6 products

Up to 10 hours of data collection

Analytical Testing

Trained human panels generate objective, reproducible sensory data for reformulation, quality control, benchmarking, and product characterization.

Descriptive Analysis (DA)

A gold-standard trained panel methodology providing detailed sensory profiles suitable for product reformulation and quality assessment.

Up to 8 trained panelists

Up to 6 products

Panel training and up to 2 hours total session time

Additional time: $800 per hour

Full Service (including panel training & lexicon development) $16,000 – $20,000
Data Collection Only $8,000

*Full service may also include new panel training and lexicon development.
